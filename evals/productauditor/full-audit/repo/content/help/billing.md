# Billing and plans

Answers to common billing questions for Fieldnote workspaces.

## Which plan am I on?

Open **Settings > Billing**. The page shows your plan, its status, and the date it renews.

## Change your plan

Open **Settings > Billing** and pick a new plan or billing period under **Change plan**. Paddle prorates the difference.

## Cancel your subscription

Open **Settings > Billing** and choose **Cancel subscription**. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, and you are not charged again.

## Update your card

Open **Settings > Billing** and choose **Update payment method**. Paddle opens a secure page where you can change the card on file.

## Get a copy of an invoice

Open **Settings > Plan & usage** and choose **Download invoices**. Every invoice for the workspace is listed there as a PDF.
